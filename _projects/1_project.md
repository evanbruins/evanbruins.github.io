I designed and fabricated an oscillating air engine, modeled to generate the highest rpm. This entailed modeling and generating engineering drawings using Solidworks, as well as doing calculations to determine engine properties. I then took the drawings I made and built it using the mill and the lathe, learning the basics of machining.

The air engine I designed to be low friction, yet with tight seals around the cylinder to maximize performance. It is dual acting, meaning air pressurization happens on both the up and down strokes of the piston. For the project, the maximum psi was 15, so I went with a smaller design for both weight reduction, and a minimized bore hole. 

These design considerations led me to take first place in the speed category with a maximum speed of over 3000 rpm for the ENGR’s 151 Air Engine Showcase.
