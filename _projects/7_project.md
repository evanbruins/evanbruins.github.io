As treasurer of the Formula Fox RC Racing Club, I constructed and iterated my custom RC car model. I sized and purchased the electrical components, but all mechanical components were designed by me. The chassis is made out of laser cut 16 gauge stainless, and tires out of rubber tube, with all the other components 3D printed out of either PLA, PETG, or TPU. 
The car is designed to be easy to fix and redesign assemblies within the model. It uses a direct drive, as 3D printing a structurally sound differential at the scale the cars are at would most likely result in minimal performance increases, with many more parts that could fail. I have updated most components from their original prototypes, to further increase their performance, and am continuing to do so as a part of the club.
